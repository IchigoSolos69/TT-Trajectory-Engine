# Research applications and limits

Reviewed 2026-10-09. This is a methods and evidence review, not a report that a newly integrated model meets the project's accuracy gates. Paper results refer to the authors' datasets, hardware, labeling conventions and evaluation protocols. They must not be substituted for measurements from this application.

The two attached PDFs were extracted without modifying the originals. Relevant figures, equations and result tables were rendered for inspection. Access evidence, text extractions and PDF hashes are in [artifacts/research_papers](artifacts/research_papers/). Three IEEE identifiers could not be reliably resolved; these remain explicitly unreviewed rather than being assigned guessed titles.

## Implementation priorities

1. **Use BlurBall as a separately identified temporal observation source.** Keep causal three-frame input in live mode. Offline use of later frames is a different method and must be reported separately. Preserve the heatmap-derived streak center, half-length and undirected axis with their method identifiers. Do not reinterpret a model score as a calibrated probability.
2. **Make contact boundaries part of trajectory fitting.** Fit observations within one flight; reset or branch at strikes, bounces and net contact. A single smooth curve across a bounce can distort position, speed and blur evidence. Predictions remain bounded and visibly distinct from observations.
3. **Calibrate geometry before claiming metric 3D or spin.** Several papers achieve 3D using synchronized, calibrated multiple cameras. A table homography alone projects elevated balls incorrectly onto the plane. Retain unknown height until an identifiable camera/flight fit supports an estimate and covariance.
4. **Evaluate adaptation on real negatives as well as visible balls.** Ceiling lights, clothing logos, shoes and hands are documented or relevant distractors. Train reviewed same-session clips together; use another complete session for independent venue generalization. A brightness filter is an experimental preprocessing candidate, not a demonstrated solution.
5. **Keep conditional spin.** Visible surface features with sufficient spatial and temporal resolution can support observed rotation. Motion-based spin requires constrained trajectory geometry and a validated model. Unmarked, tiny or aliased balls remain unresolved. Event-camera research requires different acquisition hardware.

The first three priorities belong in the authoritative C++ observation/calibration/flight representation. Next.js should display those outputs, uncertainty and reasons; it should not infer a separate trajectory or invent missing physical quantities.

## Reference ledger

### A. Attached PDFs

**A1 — BlurBall: Joint Ball and Motion Blur Estimation for Table Tennis Ball Tracking** — Thomas Gossard, Filip Radovic, Andreas Ziegler and Andreas Zell, 2025, arXiv v1. [Primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.18387v1), [official project](https://cogsys-tuebingen.github.io/blurball/), [official code](https://github.com/cogsys-tuebingen/BlurBall).

Status: complete attached 13-page PDF available; methods, evaluation, limitations and calibration supplement inspected. This is the paper for the user's BlurBall repository, not an additional independent detector.

- Three RGB frames and joint position/blur outputs directly address small blurred balls. Its dataset has 64,119 frames from 26 recordings; four recordings form its test set. The supplement excludes orange balls.
- Labels identify the **center of the blur streak**, its **half-length** `l`, and axis angle. Endpoints are `p ± l(cos θ, sin θ)`. The axis is ambiguous by 180° without temporal direction evidence; very short streak angles are unstable. A line-drawing review tool can generate this annotation with one drag and manual acceptance.
- The paper's 95 manually segmented image-plane flights compare position-only quadratic fitting against position-plus-blur fitting: MAE 84.4 ± 136.6 px versus 53.0 ± 87.1 px. This is evidence for image-plane forecasting, not monocular metric 3D accuracy.
- Exposure time is optimized jointly in that demonstration. Because the label definition is half-length while the derivative equations use `l / exposure`, implementation must resolve the length/exposure convention with synthetic tests. Do not convert a streak to physical velocity by copying that expression without a unit audit.
- Shoes, hands and logos cause false positives. A bounce occurring within an exposure violates the linear-streak assumption. Moving cameras worsen detection.
- Paper throughput on an RTX 2080 Ti and aggregate dataset F1 are historical paper measurements. Native RTX 5050 inference, causal live accuracy and complete capture-to-display latency need separate evidence.

Apply now: causal observation adapter, blur provenance, center-label review, bounded association, contact-aware fits. Needs data: venue adaptation, confidence calibration, blur-assisted physical estimation. Acceptance: fixed visible/absent frame labels, ≤4 px localization comparison at source resolution, causal startup/dropout cases, precision/recall and contact/blur subsets; record any alternative threshold rather than comparing unlike metrics.

**Inspected code constraints for fine-tuning:** the pinned upstream line heatmap generator expects angle in degrees and half-length in output-map pixels. Our reviewed axis is radians and reviewed half-length is in original source pixels; native telemetry reports full extent. The upstream dataset code transforms centers but leaves length unchanged. The implemented adapter transforms both raw endpoints once and derives correctly scaled output geometry. Its versioned `continuous_binary_capsule_v1` replaces the upstream discrete line sampling that can emit an empty map below one output pixel, retaining the original label extent. Sharp-point radius-3 parity and fractional-positive correction are tested; this intentional positive-target change is not bitwise upstream target parity or demonstrated trained accuracy. Also, the default `train_blur.yaml` selects MSE despite the paper's quality-focal method, so the adapter requires explicit loss selection. [The plan](HIGH_PRECISION_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md) records the 31-test log, source-integrity gates and visually inspected synthetic previews. Complete trainer dependencies, reviewed independent sessions and native candidate evaluation still precede fine-tuning/promotion; the installed environment does not yet provide usable `torchvision.transforms` for a full original training-runner smoke.

**A2 — Video-Based Table Tennis Tracking and Trajectory Prediction Using Convolutional Neural Networks** — Haoxuan Li and colleagues, *Fractals* 30(5), 2240156, 2022. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218348X22401569), [author institutional manuscript](https://eprints.whiterose.ac.uk/id/eprint/194374/1/s0218348x22401569.pdf).

Status: complete attached 23-page open-access PDF available; structured-output CNN, probabilistic predictor and experiment sections inspected.

- Structured-output CNN localization is followed by an LSTM and mixture-density predictor. Two Gaussian components allow flight and rebound possibilities instead of forcing one deterministic continuation.
- The real acquisition uses **two stationary, synchronized high-speed cameras**, calibrated for 3D. Training includes 20,000 Bullet3 simulated trajectories and limited real high-speed recordings. This is not a pretrained drop-in for one iPhone camera with unknown height.
- The tracking precision at 20 px and trajectory landing errors use different targets/tolerances from BlurBall's small-pixel localization; the numbers cannot be ranked directly. Reported CNN timing on GTX 1060 and LSTM timing on an i7-6700 exclude this application's complete pipeline.
- Its probabilities are not evidence of calibrated uncertainty coverage. Synthetic rebound accuracy does not establish generalization to actual racket contacts, friction, spin or a new venue.

Apply now: keep contact-specific flights and distributions in the data model. Research later: small recurrent/mixture predictor trained on audited time-stamped trajectories. Acceptance: compare against Kalman and physical baselines on held-out sessions; report horizon-specific ADE/FDE, bounce timing, landing error, absent-ball behavior, uncertainty coverage and latency. Preserve real timestamps when frame rate varies.

### C. Related verified primary research

**Table Tennis Ball Spin Estimation with an Event Camera** — Thomas Gossard, Julian Krismer, Andreas Ziegler, Jonas Tebbe and Andreas Zell, CVPR Workshops 2024. [CVF paper and PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024W/CVsports/html/Gossard_Table_Tennis_Ball_Spin_Estimation_with_an_Event_Camera_CVPRW_2024_paper.html).

Complete open-access PDF available. It isolates ball-logo events using ordinal time surfaces and estimates spin from optical flow. Reported flying-ball errors are 10.7 ± 17.3 rotations/s in magnitude and 32.9 ± 38.2° in axis. Useful future hardware path and evidence for conditional spin, not a preprocessing filter or a conventional-camera weight file. This is an additional reference, not a confirmed identification of B5/B6/B8.

## Concrete work packages and gates

| Work package | Implementation direction | Evidence required before release |
|---|---|---|
| Temporal detector | C++ causal three-frame adapter with correct color order, normalization, chronological channels and latest-frame output. Give each observation the model hash, time support and center-label convention. | Native-versus-Python parity; source-frame alignment; visible/absent precision/recall; startup and seek/disconnect reset; no future frames in live inputs. |
| Blur and easier annotation | Review one streak with a drag to its two ends; derive center and half-length while retaining raw endpoints. Suggestions require explicit acceptance. Keep person/table annotation independent and optional when only ball training is being prepared. | Reviewed label audit, corrected-frame persistence, unknown/occluded handling, no pseudo-labels silently promoted, session-grouped split and duplicate audit. |
| CNN training adapter | Implemented: audited three-consecutive-frame JSONL loader with original JPEG paths/source IDs, correctly transformed endpoint targets, explicit target/loss policy and retained provenance. | Synthetic source/time/hash alignment, geometry/tiny-target and tuple checks pass. Real independent session manifests, sufficient reviewed supervision, full trainer smoke, frozen validation/test protocol and native candidate export/causal comparisons remain required. |
| Adaptive lighting | Preserve raw footage. Compare mild luminance/contrast candidates and raw input using the same model and labels; include lights, white walls and ball absences. Train exposure variation rather than assuming dimming will restore missing spatial detail. | Precision/recall and localization by lighting/blur bin, absent-ball false positives, preprocessing/export parity and actual stage latency. Roll back if precision falls. |
| Geometry | Stabilized independent landmarks with a common coordinate frame; separate plane mapping from optional physical pose. Intrinsics, lens distortion and camera movement remain explicit. | Independent landmark errors and camera-motion/dropout tests; physical-position reference for any 3D claim; no self-fit four-corner error presented as independent accuracy. |
| Flights and contact events | Short-window weighted motion fits within a flight, provisional contact candidates, confirmed events retaining original time, bounded missing-ball propagation. | Labeled one-to-one event matching within 50 ms; measured/predicted/reacquired localization; no false contacts from tracker jumps; fit-window provenance for strike speeds. |
| Spin and counterfactuals | Surface-feature observation first; constrained trajectory/bounce fit only when identifiable. Preserve unknowns. Shared C++ simulator with explicit hypotheses and contact dynamics. | Synthetic known rotation plus real independent reference; uncertainty/aliasing tests; parameter-isolation and repeated-bounce convergence; scenario labels distinct from observed telemetry. |
| Learned forecasting | Optional small temporal/mixture model after reliable labeled trajectories exist. Compare deterministic/physical/Kalman baselines before complexity increases. | Causal held-out-session ADE/FDE and uncertainty coverage; short-horizon bounded false recovery; synthetic-to-real evaluation; native latency and memory. |

Use the existing release gates in the action plan: precision-first accepted recovery, empirical 95% region coverage, fixed event tolerances and complete-frame recording accounting. Paper architecture does not waive these gates. Candidate promotion still needs reviewed live room clips and real table rallies from independent sessions.
