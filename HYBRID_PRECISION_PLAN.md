- Complete the compatible PyTorch/torchvision trainer environment and real forward/loss/backward smoke. - After the hybrid passes acceptance, train a three-frame compact student using reviewed heatmap/absence targets plus a versioned teacher-distillation loss. Retain visibility and streak outputs. Teacher agreement may be a training signal, never an independent test reference or an automatically reviewed label. Compare supervised-only and distilled students against the unchanged teachers.
- Export accepted candidates to ONNX/TensorRT, verify source-coordinate and numerical parity, then repeat native causal evaluation. The compact model becomes selectable only if it retains the hybrid's accepted accuracy and improves measured runtime/memory; otherwise ship the validated hybrid and keep the student experimental.
